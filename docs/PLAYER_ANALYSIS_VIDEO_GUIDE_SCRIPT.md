# Game-Changrs Player Analysis Video Guide

This script matches the narrated HTML walkthrough in:

- `public/analytics-samples/player-analysis-video-guide.html`

## Scene 1
Title: Start at Game-Changrs

Voiceover:
Welcome to Game-Changrs Analytics. In this quick guide, I’ll show you how a selector or coach can use Player Assessment, and how a captain or coach can use Player Intelligence to plan against an opponent player.

## Scene 2
Title: Sign in

Voiceover:
Start on the Game-Changrs home page and click Sign In. You can log in with Google, or enter your email and password to access your account.

## Scene 3
Title: Open Analytics

Voiceover:
Once you’re signed in, open Analytics from your dashboard. This is your entry point into series search, reports, and player-level decision support.

## Scene 4
Title: Go to Series Workspace

Voiceover:
Inside Analytics, open the Series Workspace. This takes you into the series you have access to, so your search and reports stay tied to the right competition and player pool.

## Scene 5
Title: Select a series

Voiceover:
Choose the active series you want to work in, then click Use This Series. Once selected, Game-Changrs loads the tracked players, computed matches, and available coverage for that series.

## Scene 6
Title: Search the player

Voiceover:
Now search for the player you want to review. The search result card gives you the player name, team, role profile, and quick confidence signals before you open a report.

## Scene 7
Title: Open Player Assessment

Voiceover:
If you are a selector or coach assessing your own player, open Player Assessment. This view helps with selection and development by summarizing role fit, current series impact, readiness, and the player’s broader support profile.

## Scene 8
Title: Open Player Intelligence

Voiceover:
If you are a captain or coach planning against an opposition player, open Player Intelligence. This report translates the data into action: the player’s main threat, main weakness, pressure triggers, peak phase, and the practical matchups your team should plan for.

## Scene 9
Title: Choose the right report

Voiceover:
Use Player Assessment when the question is, should we back this player, develop this player, or select this player? Use Player Intelligence when the question is, how do we bowl to this batter, how do we bat against this bowler, and what is the clearest strength-and-weakness plan going into the game.
