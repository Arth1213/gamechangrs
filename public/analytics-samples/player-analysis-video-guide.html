How to use Player Assessment and Player Intelligence

This guide walks a user from login to the exact analytics decision point. It shows when to use Player Assessment as a selector or coach, and when to use Player Intelligence as a captain or coach planning against an opponent player.

Player Assessment

Selector and coaching view

Use this when the question is: is this player ready, what role does the player fit, and how should the player be backed or developed?

  • Selection support
  • Development framing
  • Role and readiness context
Player Intelligence

Captain and opposition view

Use this when the question is: where is the danger, where is the weakness, and how should the team plan against this player in a match?

  • Main threat and weakness
  • Pressure cues and matchups
  • Phase-based planning signals

Use Player Assessment
For selectors and coaches

Choose this report when you are evaluating your own player for selection, development, readiness, or role clarity.

  • Should we select this player?
  • How ready is the player right now?
  • What should the development focus be?
Use Player Intelligence
For captains and opposition coaches

Choose this report when the team needs a practical plan against an opposition player’s main strength and main weakness.

  • How should we bowl to this batter?
  • How should we bat against this bowler?
  • What matchup and pressure plan gives us the edge?
Decision rule
Assess your player. Plan against their player.

That is the cleanest split: Player Assessment is for backing, selecting, and developing your own player. Player Intelligence is for preparing against an opponent by understanding the player’s threat, weakness, and match-plan cues.
