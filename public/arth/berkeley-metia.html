The Project

GlycoStep

Adaptive pressure relief for diabetic neuropathy

GlycoStep is a three-layer smart-insole concept that combines pressure sensing, adjustable pneumatic pods, and live app feedback. The system is designed to detect pressure hotspots and actively redistribute pressure before unnoticed foot damage progresses.

Arth's Contribution Two-phase market entry, FDA pathway framing, patent strategy, investor ask, and final close
Business Model Starter kits, pharmacy distribution, and recurring replacement components
Expert Discussion Team discussion with UC Berkeley Professor Dorian Liepmann of Bioengineering and Mechanical Engineering
